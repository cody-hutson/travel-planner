**When to take a taxi (no guilt):**

### 🥚 Breakfast — Tsukiji Outer Market (IS the main event)

Wander, buy things, eat standing, buy more things. Covered inner lanes stay reasonably cool. **Strategy:** start at the back lanes away from Harumi-dori. The deeper alleys are the real finds.

- **Tamagoyaki skewer** at Marutake or Tsukuro — sweet rolled egg, watch them make it
- **Grilled scallops** — always cooked, always good, zero confrontation
- **Fresh tuna on rice** — proper Tsukiji tuna at 8 AM (hesitant raw-fish people can skip)
- **Taiyaki** — fish-shaped waffle with red bean or custard
- **Dashi maki tamago sandwiches** from the egg shops — not what you expect

Budget ¥800–2,000 per person grazing. Eat before 9:30 AM; the crowds arrive after 10.
